# Buildability study: 4 Rue Joliot-curie, 91190 Gif-sur-Yvette (parcel 91272000CR0247)

Program: Résidence Sociale Étudiante and Résidence Jeunes Actifs (two buildings), zone UMG.

## 1. Conclusion

**Verdict.** Both buildings are conditional (provisional). Résidence Sociale Étudiante (RSE): admissibility_status: conditional (provisional). Résidence Jeunes Actifs (RJA): admissibility_status: conditional (provisional). Neither use is prohibited by UMG Article 1 [c-25], but the règlement does not settle whether either building counts as habitation, and that classification sets the height and parking norms.

**Envelope.** The screening runs give a buildable volume between 63,615.1 m³ (run A3, lowest) and 112,947.6 m³ (run B1, highest) [c-48]. These are screening estimates with respect to setbacks only. They do not include the distance between the two buildings, espace vert, pleine terre, or on-site parking.

**Controlling envelope rules.** The habitation height is 20 m with R+5 [c-31], against 18 m for other destinations [c-30]. The recul from the alignment must be at least 1 m [c-34]. Side setbacks are 6 m for façades with views [c-35] and 3 m for blank façades [c-36]. The inter-building distance is 10 m where views are present [c-38] and 6 m where they are not [c-39].

**Open points that block a final answer.** The règlement leaves open:

- whether the RSE counts as habitation (Q03);
- whether the RJA counts as habitation, meaning logement (Q04), or as hébergement hôtelier, including résidences services (Q05);
- the floor-height and level-counting assumption behind R+5;
- the legal force of the OAP objectives: the R+4 average height objective (Q23), whether that objective is mandatory or a non-binding aim (Q26), and whether the alignment principle is mandatory or an orientation (Q27);
- whether a road-widening runs along the frontage, as identified in OAP section 8 for rue Joliot-Curie (Q60) and as recorded in the emplacement réservé list for the north-south axis (Q61);
- the mismatch between the 2026 metadata and the 2021 PDF version, meaning the approval or modification date printed on the UMG pages (Q62).

**Feasibility.** A developer must plan for parking, including 85% on-site integration for habitation; a two-way access road at least 5 m wide; underground networks and a sewer connection; espace vert of 20% and pleine terre of 10% of the terrain; and an enclosed refuse space for habitation above three logements. Section 6 gives the detail.

The candidate categories for both buildings are habitation (Q01 and Q02 test admissibility), hébergement hôtelier (including résidences services), and the autre destination residual. Q01 asks whether Article 1 prohibits habitation; Q02 asks whether Article 2 admits habitation under conditions.

Several points support not excluding either building. Article 1 prohibits agricultural, forestry and entrepôt buildings [c-25], and a caravane used as housing [c-26]. Neither prohibition covers a residential building. Article 2 admits only listed conditional uses, and that list has no habitation item [c-27]. No Article 2 condition applies to student residences in UMG [c-28]. The student-accommodation condition belongs to zone UL2, not UMG [c-29].

No text places the RSE under hébergement hôtelier, and this study does not assume it. The autre destination residual applies the 18 m rule only if a building is neither habitation nor service public [c-30]. Q06 asks whether Article 2 attaches any condition to student residences; the answer is no, as stated in [c-28] and [c-29].

**Why the status is conditional.** Both buildings are admitted in principle, but the category that sets the height and parking norms is open for each one. Both receive the same status because the evidence is the same for both. The RSE has indirect habitation signals [c-21, c-22], while the RJA has none [c-23]. That difference shapes the planner question but does not change the status.

## 5. Buildable envelope

**Height.** For a principal habitation destination, the limit is 20 m, with a number of levels limited to R+5 [c-31]. Q07 asks for the metric cap and Q08 for the level cap. For other destinations, the height is 18 m [c-30]. Height is measured from the sol naturel existant avant travaux [c-32], which is the ground reference asked about in Q10. The text does not define the R+5 notation beyond the notation itself [c-33].

The metric and level caps are read as cumulative, so the lower applicable cap binds. Both the N-level and N+1-level readings are run. The N+1 reading is an analyst assumption.

**Setbacks.** The recul from the alignment must be at least 1 m [c-34], which is the value asked about in Q11. Façades with views must sit at least 6 m from a limite séparative [c-35] (Q14). Blank façades must sit at least 3 m from it [c-36] (Q15). Balconies and oriels count toward the retrait [c-37] (Q16). Which façades carry views is a design fact that the text does not decide.

**Inter-building distance.** On one property, the distance is at least 10 m where at least one facing façade has views [c-38] (Q18). Where neither facing façade has views, it is at least 6 m [c-39] (Q19). The Article 8.2 exemption covers only technical and public-service equipment [c-40] (Q20).

**Emprise and stepbacks.** The emprise au sol is not regulated [c-41] (Q21). The façade recesses in Article 6.3 are a design aim, not an envelope rule [c-42] (Q22).

**Overlay carve-outs.** No confirmed emplacement réservé or overlay overlaps the parcel [c-13] to [c-18]. The overlay_carveouts input was therefore empty, and nothing was subtracted.

**OAP classification.** The règlement requires projects to be compatible with the OAP orientations. Q25 asks what legal relationship the règlement requires.

- **Binding:** Projects must be compatible with the OAP orientations [c-43]. This is tagged BINDING, as a compatibility standard set by the règlement.
- **Orientation (advisory):** The R+4 average épannelage is worded "devra permettre d'atteindre un objectif de R+4 moyen" [c-44]. This is tagged ORIENTATION (provisional; Q26 ambiguous; not used to set any envelope figure). Q23 gives the objective and Q26 asks whether it is mandatory.
- **Orientation (advisory):** The alignment principle "doit être retenu" [c-45] is tagged UNRESOLVED. Q27 asks whether it is mandatory or an orientation, and its legal force is not settled.

**Calculation.** The envelope tool was called eight times, using the WKT polygon from the confirmed context, an empty overlay_carveouts list, setbacks from Q11, Q14 and Q15, and heights from Q07 and Q09. The base run A1 trace reads [c-46]:

**Volume range.** The lowest volume is run A3, at 63,615.1 m³. The highest is run B1, at 112,947.6 m³. The lower end assumes habitation at R+5 with 3.0 m floors. The higher end assumes 1 m setbacks on every edge with a 20 m metric height. Which height applies depends on the open classification (Q03, Q04, Q05).

**What no run models.** The table below gives each omission and the direction in which it pushes the result.

- **Per-edge setbacks.** The as-extracted runs erode every edge by 6 m, although the frontage needs only 1 m. They therefore understate the footprint along frontage edges. The relaxed runs erode every edge by 1 m, so they overstate the footprint along side edges that need 6 m or 3 m. The true footprint lies between runs A and B.
- **Distance between the two buildings** (see [c-38], [c-39]). Not modelled. Two buildings reduce the buildable area, so the runs overstate it.
- **Espace vert 20% and pleine terre 10%** (see [c-73], [c-74]). Not modelled. These areas cannot carry the footprint, so the runs overstate it.
- **Surface parking and on-site parking integration.** Surface parking does not count toward espace vert (see [c-75]). The 85% on-site parking integration for habitation (see [c-55]) is not modelled. Surface parking placed on the ground therefore overstates the footprint.
- **Levels.** The tool assumes 3.0 m per floor. Taller floors reduce what fits under a level cap, so the level-capped runs overstate capacity. The N+1 reading enlarges the height and understates the constraint if R+5 means five storeys.
- **Balconies and recesses.** Not modelled. Balconies count toward the retrait (see [c-37]), so the runs may overstate usable floor area.

## 6. Other feasibility conditions

The conditions below do not change the envelope figures in section 5. They are reported separately.

- For résidences étudiantes, the basement majority is not required [c-56] (Q37).

- Shared car parks may be admitted for several operations [c-58] (Q41).

- **Orientation (advisory):** The OAP asks for underground parking or parking within the building envelope [c-62] (Q42), and for a reduced number of on-street spaces [c-63] (Q43). Its compatibility-binding status was not confirmed (Q42, partial).

- Access must allow fire and civil-protection means [c-67] (Q47).
- Refuse collection is one criterion within the general road-suitability rule [c-68] (Q48).

**Servicing**

- Each new construction must connect to the collective sewer by an underground pipe [c-69] (Q49).

- Habitation above three logements needs an enclosed space for sorting containers [c-72] (Q52).

**Ground area**

- Pleine terre is land free of any infrastructure and superstructure [c-76].

- Whether the two buildings are assessed together for these ratios is ambiguous (see section 7, item 7).

## 7. Assumptions and open points

1. **Classification of the RSE (Q03, partial).** The règlement does not define the RSE, and the habitation reading rests on indirect signals [c-21, c-22]. This choice decides between the 20 m and R+5 norm and the 18 m norm. It also decides the parking and bicycle norms.
2. **Classification of the RJA (Q04 and Q05, not found).** No text in plu_reglement names the Résidence Jeunes Actifs [c-23, c-24]. Its height, parking and admissibility remain provisional.
3. **Level counting and floor height (Q08).** The R+5 notation is not defined in the text [c-33]. The tool assumes 3.0 m per floor. Runs A3 and B3 depend on that assumption, and runs A4 and B4 are the analyst N+1 reading.
4. **Per-building parking cap (Q34, partial).** Whether the L. 631-12 cap applies depends on item 1 [c-54].
5. **Bicycle norm for the RSE (Q38, partial) and the scope of the 3 m² area (Q39).** Both are open for the RSE [c-59, c-60].
6. **Whether the RSE and RJA form one operation or two (Q40).** The per-destination sum depends on the classification [c-57].
7. **Espace vert and pleine terre per building (Q56, ambiguous).** Q56 asks whether these percentages are computed on the whole terrain rather than per construction. The text uses "la superficie du terrain" [c-81], but the UMG text contains no rule saying whether the percentages apply to the whole terrain or per building. The scope read was UMG rappels pdf p.320 and Article 13 pdf p.331, with no "plusieurs bâtiments" or "chacun des lots" wording. The whole-terrain reading is an interpretation.
8. **Refuse collection and emergency access geometry (Q47, Q48).** The règlement sets only general road suitability [c-68].
9. **Legal force of the OAP (Q23, Q26, Q27).** **Orientation (advisory):** The OAP does not state the legal effect of its orientations. A full-text search for compatib, conformit, opposable, prévaut and portée juridique found no statement of binding force [c-82] (Q27). The alignment and R+4 items stay UNRESOLVED or ORIENTATION as tagged in section 5.
10. **OAP parking compatibility (Q42, partial).** The compatibility-binding premise is not confirmed [c-62].
11. **OAP section 9 (Q28: whether the OAP contains a section numbered 9).** The règlement cites orientations 8 et 9 [c-85]. The OAP table of contents ends with "8. Le quartier Joliot Curie" [c-84], and a full-document search found no section 9 [c-83]. This gap is unresolved and may reflect the version mismatch.
12. **Road-widening along rue Joliot-Curie (Q60) and the north-south ER (Q61).** **Orientation (advisory):** The OAP describes the rue Joliot-Curie as requalifiée [c-86], with a gabarit of about 20 m [c-87]. The ER élargissements table has no Joliot-Curie or north-south entry [c-88]. A human should check the red-arrow ER against the plan.
13. **Version mismatch (Q62).** The UMG pages carry the footer "Modification n°1 et révision allégée n°1 approuvées le 6 juillet 2021" [c-89]. The cover lists the same approvals [c-91]. The metadata zone name refers to reglement_20260408.pdf, and the string 2026 does not occur in plu_reglement [c-90]. A human should check which version governs.
14. **Recommandations sanitaires versus OAP perimeter (undetermined).** The regime is absent from the UMG text [c-19], but the dashed purple line cannot be separated from the magenta OAP line on the map.
15. **Recensed elements under L.151-19 and L.151-23 (undetermined; Q58: whether UMG imposes conditions near such elements).** The UMG text contains no condition on construction near such elements [c-94]. The UA2 zone does contain such a condition [c-96], and the general demolition permit covers recensed built elements [c-95]. Presence on the parcel is not established, because plan n°5.5 is only a thumbnail.
16. **Acoustic corridor (Q57).** Whether the parcel lies in a corridor depends on the pièce n°8 graphic [c-79, c-80].
17. **Espace vert and pleine terre relation (Q54).** The règlement says at least 50% of required espaces végétalisés must be pleine terre [c-97]. How this combines with the 10% floor is not resolved.
18. **BD 247 entries (Q59: whether any ER listing refers to CR 0247).** Two ER rows list BD 247, which is a different section from CR 0247 [c-99]. No link to CR 0247 is assumed.
19. **Sector boundary (Q63: whether OAP section 8 describes the sector limits in text).** OAP section 8 does not describe the sector's limits in text. The only textual "limite" refers to the Gif-sur-Yvette/Orsay commune boundary [c-92]. The sector perimeter appears only graphically [c-93].
20. **Carry-forward limitations.** These are known gaps that text retrieval cannot close [c-98]:
    - The zone and OAP rely on visual reading of soft zoom imagery. The calls are robust because the nearest boundaries are far from the parcel.
    - Table 04 contains no cadastral references. A red-arrow nord-sud ER appears in the legend, but its position cannot be matched to a table row. A human should confirm that no road-widening ER runs along the frontage.
    - The sub-sector boundary of the Quartier Joliot Curie OAP is not drawn separately in the zoom images. A human should confirm that the magenta outline matches the OAP sector outline.
    - The recommandations sanitaires (dashed purple) and the OAP perimeter (magenta dotted) look similar at this resolution. The recommandations check is undetermined.
    - The L 151-19 and L 151-23 element check is undetermined, because 02_elements_recenses.png shows only a thumbnail of plan n°5.5.
    - The west distance from the parcel to the OAP perimeter is not stated. A human should measure it on the plan.

## 8. How conflicts were resolved

Where the planner's inputs conflicted with the source, the source was preferred. Each resolution is recorded below.

- **Q02 (contradicts planner).** The planner said the zone introduction supports habitation without conditions. Winner: the operative text. The introduction is descriptive, and Article 2 is the operative list. Habitation admission still rests on the absence of a prohibition, so the status stays provisional.
- **Q09 (contradicts planner).** The planner gave hébergement hôtelier or résidences services as examples of the autre destination case. Winner: the source, because Article 10.2 names no residence type and Q04 and Q05 were not found. The 18 m rule is used as a run, not as a classification.
- **Q13 (contradicts planner; Q13 is the implantation principle OAP section 8 sets along rue Joliot-Curie).** The planner said OAP section 8 may refine UMG Article 6. Winner: the source, because the OAP does not reference Article 6.
- **Q34 (contradicts planner).** The planner said the parking obligation may not apply to a résidence universitaire. Winner: the text, which caps the requirement at one space per logement and does not disapply it.
- **Q40 (contradicts planner).** The planner framed the per-destination sum as a sum across two buildings. Winner: the text, which refers to one operation with several destinations.
- **Q42 and Q43 (contradict planner).** The planner said the on-street limit was separate from the underground guidance and that OAP parking is compatibility-binding. Winner: the OAP text, which places both items in one bullet, and no compatibility-binding statement was found.
- **Q47 (contradicts planner).** The planner tied fire access to both buildings. Winner: Article 3.2, which applies to all constructible terrains.
- **Q48 (contradicts planner).** The planner called refuse access a separate condition. Winner: Article 3.2, which places waste collection inside the general road-suitability criterion.
- **Q50 (contradicts planner).** The planner said the rule sets a volume. Winner: the text, which sets a discharge rate of 0.7 L/s/ha and no volume.
- **Q54 (contradicts planner).** The planner said pleine terre is stricter than espace vert. Winner: the thresholds, which give pleine terre 10% against espace vert 20%. Pleine terre is therefore the lower floor.
- **Q55 (contradicts planner).** The planner said the tree rule applies to the parcel and must be shown on a landscape plan. Winner: the text, which makes the rule conditional on terrain above 300 m² and on new construction, and which requires no landscape plan. Applicability is open.
- **Q56 (contradicts planner; Q56 asks whether the espace vert and pleine terre tests are computed on the whole terrain).** The planner called the test a single whole-terrain test. Winner: the text, which uses "superficie du terrain" but has no multi-building rule, so the reading is interpretive.
- **Q58 (contradicts planner).** The planner said zone UL2 contains the L 151-19 and L 151-23 wording. Winner: the UMG text, which has no such condition. The wording found sits in UA2, not UL2.
- **Overlap check.** No emplacement réservé overlaps the parcel, so no overlay carve-out was passed to the tool.
